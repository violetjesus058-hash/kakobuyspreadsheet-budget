# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Kakobuy Spreadsheet Budget** ("we," "us," or "this site") at [kakobuyspreadsheet-budget.com](https://kakobuyspreadsheet-budget.com/) handles information. The site is an independent directory for Kakobuy spreadsheet routes, budget filters, category links, brand shortcuts, QC references, and shopping notes.

## 1. Independent directory role

Kakobuy Spreadsheet Budget does not sell physical products or operate the external catalog. We do not process third-party orders or payments, operate warehouses, arrange shipping, issue refunds, or provide after-sales service. External destinations handle those matters. Brand names, product images, and platform references belong to their respective rights holders and do not imply official authorization, authenticity, quality assurance, or endorsement.

## 2. Information collected

You may browse without an account. If you contact us or submit a request form that we make available, we may collect your name, email address, message, and other information you voluntarily provide.

Technical and usage information may be collected automatically by hosting, security, and measurement systems. This can include IP address, approximate location from IP, device and operating-system information, browser and language, referring URL, landing page, clicks, navigation path, time spent, timestamps, errors, and UTM or other campaign parameters.

We or configured tags may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons, and similar technologies for essential preferences, performance, security, traffic analysis, and outbound-click or referral attribution. This site does not use them to run an account, accept payment, or complete checkout.

## 3. Uses

We use information to publish and maintain Kakobuy navigation routes; make budget, category, brand, and guide links more relevant; understand traffic sources and campaign performance; measure outbound clicks and referrals; detect automated abuse, click fraud, scraping, and attacks; improve performance and accessibility; troubleshoot errors; and comply with legal duties. Any personalized measurement or recommendation is controlled by the relevant configured tag and applicable law.

## 4. Google Tag Manager and other providers

This site directly loads **Google Tag Manager container GTM-5M5VQ9MW**. Google Tag Manager can load measurement or attribution tags that are actually configured in the current container. We do not represent that Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or other SDKs are active unless the configuration enables them.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's Tag Manager information](https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/6102821). Hosting, security, domain, and performance providers may process limited technical information and may use international infrastructure.

## 5. Sharing and external links

We may share limited technical, aggregated, or pseudonymous information with providers that host, secure, measure, or maintain this directory. For referral attribution, a third-party destination may receive the referring URL, campaign or UTM values, click time, and ordinary browser-request information. We do not sell names, phone numbers, or email addresses collected through this directory.

After you leave this site or open an external app, the destination independently determines how it collects and uses information. Its privacy policy covers accounts, cookies, SDKs, payments, orders, warehouse activity, shipping, refunds, and support. Review that policy before submitting information. We are not responsible for third-party practices.

## 6. Storage, retention, and security

Information may be processed or stored by our providers in the countries where their infrastructure operates. We do not promise one fixed storage location for every provider. We retain information only as long as needed for site operation, referral measurement, security, legal compliance, or dispute handling, then seek to delete or anonymize it when practical. Provider settings and legal obligations may require longer retention.

Reasonable safeguards include HTTPS/TLS, data minimization, access controls, restricted administrative access, and security logging. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If we identify a material breach, we will investigate, contain it, and provide legally required notices.

## 7. Rights, cookies, and contact

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection; withdraw consent; or ask us to stop personalized recommendations or referral measurement. You may disable or delete cookies and local storage through browser settings, use private browsing, and adjust available advertising controls. Some measurement functions may not work after blocking cookies.

Send a privacy request to **privacy@kakobuyspreadsheet-budget.com** with the subject "Privacy Request." We may verify your request and will respond within the period required by law. A privacy or contact link on this site may also be used when available.

## 8. Children's privacy

This directory is designed for adults and does not knowingly solicit or intentionally collect children's personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent information to us, contact **privacy@kakobuyspreadsheet-budget.com**. After reasonable verification, we will delete it when required and appropriate.

## 9. Changes and contact scope

We may revise this Policy when the directory, providers, tracking configuration, or law changes. The latest version and date will be posted on this page. Contact **privacy@kakobuyspreadsheet-budget.com** for privacy questions. Send order, payment, shipping, refund, or after-sales questions to the applicable external destination, not to this directory.
